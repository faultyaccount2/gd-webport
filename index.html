About this webport · Features & FAQ

Play Geometry Dash online in your browser

This fan-made Geometry Dash webport runs the game locally through WebAssembly and WebGL2. It is a static site with no install, account, or plugin, and it keeps the original 1920×1080 logical resolution on desktop and mobile.

What is included

  • Progress saving: profiles, stats, and local levels autosave every 15 seconds and when you hide or close the tab. Saved data stays in your browser.
  • Online levels and custom music: search and download levels from the online browser, including Newgrounds songs. Online data survives a reload and resets with a new browser session.
  • Full level editor: build, test, save, and reload your own levels.
  • Desktop and mobile: keyboard, mouse, and multi-touch input, plus fullscreen and landscape support where the browser allows it.
  • Browser storage: progress and downloaded online content are kept in this browser on itch.io.

Frequently asked questions

Is Geometry Dash free to play in the browser?

Yes. This fan webport is free and needs no account, download, or payment. The first load downloads about 328 MB of game files, then the browser caches them.

Does this webport save my progress?

Yes. Normal progress is stored in localStorage and saved automatically. It is tied to the same browser and site origin. Use Reset data to clear it.

Can I play online levels and download custom music?

Yes. Online levels and their custom songs are stored in IndexedDB for the current browser session, so they survive a reload but are cleared when a new browser session starts. Your normal progress is not affected.

Does it include the level editor?

Yes, the full editor is included. You can place objects, test a level, save it locally, and continue editing after a reload.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The game starts windowed so the on-screen pause, arrow, and keyboard controls stay available. Tap the fullscreen button if you want more room — the controls remain visible there too. Multi-touch is supported, and landscape gives the largest view.

What browser do I need?

Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari with WebAssembly, WebGL2, and gzip DecompressionStream support. Older browsers show a clear update message instead of downloading the engine.
